# NCAA FBS playing-rules registry, 2014–2025

Compiled 2026-10-02. Machine-readable version: `config/rules_registry.json`. This file covers only rules that change scoring, possession, the game clock or how a game ends.

Every entry below comes from a document that was opened and read. Most citations are to the official NCAA *Football Rules and Interpretations* books. Where a season's book was not available, the entry uses the official rules-change list for that season instead. Rule numbers refer to the NCAA rulebook.

## Changes by season

| Season | Area | Change (in force from this season) | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2014 | (baseline) | Rules as in the 2013–14 book. OT: possessions from the 25 in every period; two-point try mandatory after a TD from the **3rd** OT; no shootout. First down stops the clock all game (restarts on the ready-for-play signal). Out of bounds: clock restarts on the ready signal, or on the snap in the last 2:00 of a half. 10-second runoff only in the final 1:00. Fair catch of a free kick: ball at the spot of the catch | 3-1-3, 3-3-2, 3-4-4, 6-5-1 | S1 |
| 2015 | — | Nothing that affects these items (officials' crew of 8, helmet-off play clock, sideline warning, replay of onside-kick blocking) | — | S12 (secondary); S1 and S2 have identical text |
| 2016 | Clock | If the clock is stopped only to complete a penalty for a foul by the team **ahead** in the last 2:00 of a half, the offended team may choose to start the clock on the snap | 3-4-3 | S2 |
| 2017 | — | No change found (the 2016–17 book covers both seasons) | — | S2 |
| 2018 | Kickoff | A fair catch of a free kick behind the receiving team's 25 is put in play at the 25 | 6-5-1-a | S3 |
| 2018 | Scoring/end of game | A team that scores a TD as time expires in Q4 and leads by 1 or 2 may skip the try (before 2018: the try is skipped unless it could affect the result) | 8-3-2-a | S3 |
| 2018 | Clock | 40-second play clock after free kicks and touchdowns; replay can trigger a 10-second runoff | 3-2-4, 12-3-6 | S3 |
| 2019 | Overtime | From the **5th** OT, each possession is a single two-point play from the 3 (OT 1–4 unchanged; two-point try still mandatory from the 3rd OT). 2-minute mandatory break after the 2nd and 4th OT if there is no media timeout | 3-1-3-e, 3-1-3-h | S4, S5 |
| 2020 | Clock | If replay restores time at the end of a half and the clock would start on the referee's signal, at least 3 seconds must remain | 12-3-6 | S6 |
| 2020 | Overtime | A foul whose penalty can carry over to the next kickoff can also carry over to the next spot in overtime | 10-2-5 | S6 |
| 2021 | Overtime | Two-point try mandatory after a TD from the **2nd** OT. From the **3rd** OT, each possession is a single two-point play from the 3 | 3-1-3-e/f | S7 |
| 2022 | Possession | A fumble that comes to rest ahead of the fumble spot belongs to the fumbling team at the fumble spot. Defensive holding always gives an automatic first down. Illegal touching adds loss of down | 7-2-5-b, 9-3-4, 7-3-11 | S8 |
| 2023 | Clock | A first down stops the clock **only in the last 2:00 of Q2 and Q4** (restarts on the ready-for-play signal) | 3-3-2-e-1 | S9 |
| 2023 | End of period | Untimed down for an accepted foul / offsetting fouls / inadvertent whistle **only at the end of Q2 and Q4** | 3-2-3-a | S9 |
| 2023 | Timeouts | A team may not take consecutive charged timeouts in the same dead-ball period | 3-3-4 | S9 |
| 2024 | Clock | **Two-Minute Timeout**: the clock stops at 2:00 of Q2 and Q4 (or when the ball becomes dead if a play is live at 2:00). Every "under 2:00" trigger now applies "after the Two-Minute Timeout": first-down stop, out-of-bounds restart on the snap, clock-on-snap penalty option (now also either team when tied), 10-second runoff (widened from the final 1:00) | 3-3-5, 3-3-2-d/e, 3-4-3, 3-4-4 | S10 |
| 2024 | Halftime | No further replay review of the last play after the referee declares the first half over | 3-2-1-c | S10 |
| 2025 | Overtime | Timeouts: 1 per team in OT1, 1 in OT2, then 1 per team for all of OT3 onward. Radio/TV timeouts only after OT1 and OT2; mandatory break only after OT2 | 3-1-3-h | S11 |
| 2025 | Clock | Two-Minute Timeout explicitly listed as "clock starts on the snap". After the Two-Minute Timeout, a defensive 12-men foul (extra player took part in the play) lets the offended team reset the clock to the time at the snap | 3-3-2-d-12, 3-5-3-b | S11 |

### Overtime format summary

| Seasons | Start | Two-point try mandatory from | Two-point-play-only periods from | Timeouts | Break |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2014–2018 | Opp. 25 | OT3 | never | 1 per period | none in rule |
| 2019–2020 | Opp. 25 (OT1–4) | OT3 | OT5 (from the 3) | 1 per period | after OT2, OT4 |
| 2021–2024 | Opp. 25 (OT1–2) | OT2 | OT3 (from the 3) | 1 per period | after OT2, OT4 |
| 2025 | Opp. 25 (OT1–2) | OT2 | OT3 (from the 3) | 1 in OT1, 1 in OT2, 1 total OT3+ | after OT2 |

In every season, a one-point kick try during a mandatory-two-point period is legal but scores nothing (3-1-3-f).

## Unverified

These items could not be confirmed directly from an opened source. Each is null or annotated in the JSON.

1. **The 2015, 2020 and 2022 rulebooks were not opened.** Values for those seasons are carried forward from the nearest book (2013–14 and 2016–17 for 2015; 2019 for 2020; 2021 for 2022) and checked against that season's change list (S12, S6, S8). A rule changed and then reversed inside one of these seasons would not be detected.
2. **Second-half choice in 2014–2015.** The 2013–14 book allows the toss winner to "defer his selection to the second half", but it lacks the explicit sentence (first seen in the 2016–17 book) giving the second-half choice to "the loser of the toss, or the winner who [deferred]". The case where the winner did not defer is not stated in S1.
3. **2017.** The 2016–17 copy read (S2) may be the original 2016 printing rather than the "updated 7/19/2017" version listed on ncaapublications.com. 2017-only mid-cycle changes, if any, were not seen.
4. **Clock restart after the 2024 Two-Minute Timeout.** The 2024 book's starts-on-snap list does not include the Two-Minute Timeout. The 2025 book adds it, and a 2025 approved ruling says the clock starts on the snap. `two_minute_timeout_clock_restart` is null for 2024.
5. **No maximum number of overtime periods.** This comes from the absence of any limit in Rule 3-1-3, not from an explicit statement.
6. **No NCAA.org news article was opened.** The 2021 NCAA.org overtime article now redirects to ncaa.org/news. The overtime changes are cited to the rulebooks and the 2019 memo instead.
7. **The 2026 rulebook (out of scope) was not used for any 2014–2025 value.**
